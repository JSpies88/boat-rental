Boat rentals

Cape Town Boat Rental

Choose a private charter, hire a bare boat for your own leisure, or hop aboard a launched boat out of Granger Bay. Use the contact form at the bottom to tell us what you'd like and when.

Launched trips

Day Trips from Granger Bay — hop on and go

Skip the launch: our boats are already in the water at Oceana Power Boat Club. Turn up, hop on and head out — ideal for quick departures, working around tight schedules, or if you simply want to save time.

  • Meet at Oceana Power Boat Club
  • Quick boarding — boats are already launched
  • Self-skippered departures: Flexible depature and returns

Bare boat hire

Need a boat for a Breakaway?

For experienced skippers: hire a boat and explore at your own pace — rivers, dams, or the coastline — wherever local regulations allow. You take the helm; we make sure the boat is ready and safe.

  • Requirements: Valid licence/experience as per rental terms
  • Flexible durations: full-day, or multi-day
  • We provide a briefing and safety equipment

Private charter

Sunrise & sunset charters — up to 6 people

Relax with a local skipper on a private charter. Perfect for small groups, celebrations, photo shoots, or a slow day on the water. Choose sunrise for calm seas and golden light, or sunset for warm skies and evening city views.

  • Capacity: Up to 6 guests
  • Includes skipper, safety kit & basic refreshments
  • Flexible start times for sunrise or sunset (up to 2 hours)
  • Departing from Oceana Power Boat Club
